Full page promptMarkdown · 17,135 characters
## Task
- Build a single-page landing page from the specification below.
- Follow the layout structure exactly: do not add, remove, merge or reorder sections, and do not add elements the specification does not mention.
- This pass builds the layout structure only. For any visual detail the specification leaves open, use the defaults under "Placeholder content and neutral styling"; do not improvise a visual style.
- Where the specification differs from common practice, follow the specification; do not fall back to the usual pattern.
- Build the whole page in one go; if you must split the output, continue from the next section heading without omitting or summarizing anything.

## Placeholder content and neutral styling
- Build the whole page with placeholder content and neutral styling first. The real copy, images, colors and fonts will be swapped in by the user afterwards.
- Use neutral placeholder text of realistic length (short headlines, two- to three-line paragraphs); do not use very short text that makes the layout look tidier than it will be.

Frequently asked questions

What is PASTEUP?

PASTEUP is a free landing-page prompt generator, built to cut down on generic AI slop. Pick a header, hero, content sections and footer from ready-made layout blocks, and the composer turns the whole layout into one complete prompt, so the AI builds the layout you chose instead of guessing.

Do I need an account or to pay?

No. Open the composer and start; there is no sign-up, and prompts copy straight to your clipboard.

Where can I use the prompt?

Paste it into any AI tool. The full-page prompt describes every block at once, or copy a single block’s prompt to replace one section of a page.

Which blocks are available?

130 blocks so far, in four groups: header, hero, content and footer. Each one comes with style notes and the media types it works with.
